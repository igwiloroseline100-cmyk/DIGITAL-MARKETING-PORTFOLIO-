CAMPAIGN THINKING

Plan → Launch → Measure → Optimize

PLAN

Define the business goal, audience, offer, campaign objective and measurement approach.

LAUNCH

Build the campaign structure, ads, targeting, placements and budget.

MEASURE

Monitor meaningful metrics and compare performance against the campaign objective.

DIAGNOSE

Identify where performance is breaking down and separate symptoms from actual causes.

OPTIMIZE

Make controlled changes based on evidence and observe the resulting performance.

REPORT

Summarize performance, insights, decisions and recommended next steps.

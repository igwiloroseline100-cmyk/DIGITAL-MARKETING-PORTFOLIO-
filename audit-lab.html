Learning to think like a technical SEO auditor.

ABOUT THIS LAB

Practice work, clearly labelled.

This section contains technical SEO investigations completed as learning and practice exercises.

These are not presented as paid client projects, real client results, or fabricated case studies.

Each investigation focuses on understanding a technical problem, examining the evidence, diagnosing the likely cause, and deciding what should be done next.

PRACTICE RESULTS

Key findings from the e-commerce investigation.

The following findings come from the practice e-commerce audit scenario investigated in the lab. They represent analysis and diagnosis, not client performance results.

Audit scenario: 25,000 URLs discovered, 8,000 indexed, 5,000 parameter variations, 1,200 404s, 300 important orphan product pages, 800 noindex pages, and 400 important product pages canonicalised elsewhere.
FINDING 01

URL Explosion

Parameter and filter variations were creating large numbers of URLs, contributing to inefficient crawling.

FINDING 02

Crawl Inefficiency

Googlebot was frequently crawling parameter URLs, indicating that crawl resources were being spent on low-value variations.

FINDING 03

Orphan Product Pages

300 important product pages lacked sufficient internal linking, creating a discoverability and architecture problem.

FINDING 04

Indexation Signals

Noindex and canonical signals required an intent review to determine whether important pages were being unnecessarily excluded or consolidated.

FINDING 05

404 Investigation

The 1,200 404 URLs should not be bulk-redirected automatically. Each should be assessed based on whether the page was removed, moved or deleted accidentally.

FINDING 06

Sitemap Quality

The sitemap should be reviewed so it contains the intended canonical, indexable and successfully responding URLs.

FINDING 07

Architecture Depth

Some important product pages were 5–7 clicks from the homepage, making internal-link structure and navigation worth reviewing.

FINDING 08

Nonindexed Does Not Mean Broken

The 17,000 discovered but nonindexed URLs were not treated as automatically problematic. Their intended indexability must be classified first.

FINDING 09

Canonical Is Not Cannibalization

Canonicalisation was evaluated as a URL consolidation signal, not automatically treated as keyword cannibalisation.

WHAT THIS BUILDS

More than a checklist.

These exercises are designed to develop the thinking required to work through real technical SEO problems.

Think in systems.

Understand how crawling, indexing, architecture, links and technical signals interact.

Prioritise problems.

Separate critical technical issues from problems that have little practical impact.

Recommend solutions.

Turn technical findings into clear, practical actions that can actually be implemented.

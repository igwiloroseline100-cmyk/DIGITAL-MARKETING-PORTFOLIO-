SEO AUDIT LAB

Learning to think like a technical SEO auditor.

Practical technical SEO investigations focused on identifying problems, understanding their causes, and developing sensible solutions.

ABOUT THIS LAB

Practice work, clearly labelled.

This section contains technical SEO investigations completed as learning and practice exercises.

These are not presented as paid client projects, real client results, or fabricated case studies.

Each investigation focuses on understanding a technical problem, examining the evidence, diagnosing the likely cause, and deciding what should be done next.

INVESTIGATIONS

Practical technical SEO exercises.

Each investigation focuses on a specific technical problem and the reasoning required to diagnose it.

01

CRAWL & INDEXATION

E-commerce Crawl Investigation

Investigated a large e-commerce scenario involving excessive URLs, parameter variations, orphan pages, noindex pages and canonical issues.

Focus: Crawl efficiency, indexation and URL management.
02

INDEXATION

Why Pages Are Not Indexed

Investigated possible reasons important pages may not appear in Google's index, including noindex directives, canonical signals, crawl problems and page-quality considerations.

Focus: Crawlability and indexability.
03

REDIRECTS & STATUS CODES

404 & Redirect Investigation

Investigated broken URLs and determined when a URL should remain a 404 or 410 and when a relevant replacement should receive a 301 redirect.

Focus: HTTP status codes and redirects.
04

DUPLICATE CONTENT

Canonical Investigation

Investigated duplicate and near-duplicate URLs to determine which version should be treated as the preferred URL.

Focus: Canonicalisation and duplicate URLs.
05

SITE ARCHITECTURE

Orphan Page Investigation

Investigated pages with little or no internal linking support and examined how weak internal connections can affect discoverability.

Focus: Internal linking and discoverability.
06

CRAWL EFFICIENCY

URL Explosion Investigation

Investigated how filters, facets and URL parameters can create large numbers of URL variations and unnecessary crawling.

Focus: Faceted navigation, parameters and crawl control.
07

CRAWL CONTROL

Robots.txt Investigation

Investigated how robots.txt controls crawling and why blocking a URL from crawling is different from preventing it from being indexed.

Focus: Crawling and indexation control.
08

URL MANAGEMENT

URL Parameter Investigation

Investigated how parameters can create multiple versions of similar URLs and how they can affect crawling, duplication and indexation.

ON-PAGE SEO

Making pages clearer for search and users.

On-Page SEO focuses on how content, page structure and search intent work together to help search engines understand what a page is about and help users find useful information.

CORE AREAS

What I work on.

On-page optimisation connects the content of a page with the needs of the person searching for it.

01

Search Intent

Understanding what users are actually trying to accomplish when they search and aligning the page with that intent.

02

Title Tags

Creating clear, relevant page titles that accurately communicate the subject of the page.

03

Meta Descriptions

Writing useful descriptions that accurately summarise the page and encourage qualified searchers to visit.

04

Headings & Structure

Organising content with logical headings and a structure that makes the page easier to understand and navigate.

05

Content Optimisation

Improving relevance, usefulness, clarity and topical coverage without forcing keywords unnaturally into content.

06

Keyword Strategy

Connecting relevant search terms with the right pages while avoiding keyword stuffing and unnecessary duplication.

07

Internal Linking

Using relevant internal links to connect related content, support navigation and strengthen site structure.

08

Image Optimisation

Optimising images through appropriate formats, dimensions, file sizes, filenames and useful alternative text.

09

URL Optimisation

Creating understandable, consistent URLs that accurately represent the subject and structure of the page.

10

Content Quality

Reviewing whether content is useful, original, trustworthy, complete enough for its purpose and genuinely helpful to users.

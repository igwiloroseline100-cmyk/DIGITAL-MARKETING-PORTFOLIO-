MY METHOD

An audit should lead to defensible decisions.

I do not treat every automated warning as a confirmed SEO problem. I check the affected URL, gather supporting evidence, assess likely impact, and separate verified findings from items that need more investigation.

01 · DISCOVER

Understand the site

Review site structure, important page types, internal links, sitemap coverage and the tools or access available for testing.

02 · VALIDATE

Verify the evidence

Check URLs, status codes, indexability signals, metadata and crawl results instead of assuming a tool warning is correct.

03 · PRIORITISE

Choose meaningful fixes

Rank confirmed findings by affected pages, likely search or user impact, confidence, implementation effort and risk.

04 · RETEST

Prove what changed

Repeat the relevant check after an approved fix and record the result, remaining limitations and any follow-up work.

AREAS OF INVESTIGATION

What I check during an audit

Crawlability and indexability

Robots directives, indexability signals, HTTP status codes, redirects and whether important pages can be accessed and indexed.

Internal links and architecture

Broken internal destinations, orphan-page risks, navigation paths, click depth and whether important pages are discoverable.

Canonicalisation and duplication

Canonical signals, duplicate URL patterns, inconsistent URL variants and alignment between internal links and preferred URLs.

Metadata and performance

Page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, mobile experience and performance measurements interpreted in context.

Evidence standard: Recommendations are not presented as completed fixes or proven ranking improvements unless the relevant change and outcome have actually been verified.

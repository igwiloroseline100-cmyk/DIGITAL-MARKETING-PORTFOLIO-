PLANNED CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE · NOT ACTIVE

What happens after checkout?

In a future live store, customers will receive order updates after payment is independently verified. This demo does not create orders or send notifications.

  1. 01

    Order received

    An actual backend would create an order reference and record the requested items.

  2. 02

    Payment verified

    A connected payment provider would confirm a successful transaction; pay-on-delivery orders would remain unpaid until payment is collected.

  3. 03

    Availability reviewed

    The business would confirm stock and prepare the order before scheduling shipping.

  4. 04

    Shipping date confirmed

    The customer would be notified once a realistic shipping date is available. No date is promised in advance.

Current status: Prototype only — no orders accepted, no payment verification and no shipping emails sent.

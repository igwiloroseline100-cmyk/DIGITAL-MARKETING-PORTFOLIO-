PRIVATE OPERATIONS · PRODUCTS

A catalogue the business can control.

The product-management system is part of the separate owner backend. This public page shows its purpose without exposing editing, deletion or privileged product access.

What the catalogue backend handles

Product management

Owner-side creation and removal of sample products with names, categories and prices.

Controlled pricing

The server uses its own catalogue prices when calculating sample order totals, rather than trusting amounts sent by a browser.

Separate permissions

Public visitors browse products. Owner operations require authenticated server-side access.

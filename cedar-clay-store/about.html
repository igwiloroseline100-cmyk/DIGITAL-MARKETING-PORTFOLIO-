OUR APPROACH

Useful design is more than decoration.

A strong online store makes it easy to understand an item, explore related products, manage a bag and complete a predictable journey.

This project combines semantic HTML, responsive CSS, dedicated product URLs and JavaScript interactions. It is an interactive prototype, not a commercial shop.

There is no real inventory, payment processing, delivery, customer account or backend order storage.
